# Blog post

Posts go on karun.me. The reader is an engineer or an engineering leader who will try what the post says.

## Post type

The brief names one of two post types, and the type sets the heading style. Show both heading sets at gate 2 either way, and recommend the one the type calls for.

- **How-to:** headings are labels or imperatives ("Run tests in parallel"). The post walks the reader through steps they can repeat.
- **Experience report:** headings are short sentences ("Each test runs in one place"). The post tells what happened and what the user learned from it.

## Shape

- **Opening:** the point, the user's situation, or the reader's problem, in the first paragraph. No warm-up and no history of the topic.
- **Closing:** a recommendation or the last practical step. No summary of the sections and no list of principles. Thanking reviewers is fine.
- **Series:** if the post is part of a series, link the posts before and after it near the top. Use the numbers and terms the sibling posts already use (the brief lists them).
- **Code samples must parse or run.** Paste real code, or code you have run. Mark a shortened sample with a comment such as `// ...`.
- **Numbers and claims** link to their source the first time they appear.
