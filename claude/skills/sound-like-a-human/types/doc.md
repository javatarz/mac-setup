# Document for publishing

A document is something a team or a client reads to decide or to act: a proposal, a guide, a write-up, or a context doc. It is read more than once and often skimmed.

## Shape

- **Open with what the reader gets from it** and what they're asked to decide or do, if anything.
- **Headings are labels.** A reader scanning the headings should find the section they need.
- **Tables for comparisons** across more than 2 options or more than 2 attributes. Prose for reasoning.
- **Decisions keep their reason and date** ("Decided 2026-11-24: ... because ...").
- **Code samples must parse or run.**
- **Branding:** "intelligent Engineering" and "emerge" are always spelled out and lowercase-first. The linter checks this.

Voice, honesty rules, and the avoid-list apply as for a post. A document is more neutral in tone but still says what the user recommends.
