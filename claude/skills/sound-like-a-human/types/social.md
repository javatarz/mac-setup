# LinkedIn or X post

A social post is read in a feed, in a few seconds. Skip the brief and both gates.

## Shape

- **The first line carries the point.** It's the only line most readers see.
- **Full stops, not qualifiers hung off a sentence.** Split a qualifier into its own sentence.
- **Numerals,** not spelled-out numbers: "3 steps", not "three steps". Also at the start of a sentence: "2 of them fired late", not "Two of them". The linter warns on 2 to 10 and on "one" used as a number.
- **No softening "but"** in short statements. This overrides the voice rule for longer writing, where a short contrast is joined with "but".
- **Link the post or page** it's about, once, at the end.
- **No hashtag lists and no calls to action** ("Thoughts?", "Agree?").

Never post. Show the final text to the user; they post it.
