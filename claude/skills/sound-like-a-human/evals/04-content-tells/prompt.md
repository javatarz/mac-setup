The engineers I admire most treat AI output as a first draft, never a final answer. They review every line, question every assumption, and hold the same bar they always have.

This approach works because it separates speed from judgment. Teams that adopt it move faster without sacrificing quality. Teams that skip it eventually pay the cost in production incidents.

The principle is simple: trust the tool to generate, trust the engineer to decide. Organizations that internalize this distinction will outperform those that treat AI as a shortcut around thinking.
