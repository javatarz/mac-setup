# Brief: <working title>

Fill this in during draft mode, step 3. The user approves it at gate 1. Every later step reads it, so record decisions here, not only in chat.

## Reader

Who reads this, what they already know, and what they'll do after reading.

## Point

The one thing the piece says, in one or two sentences.

## Type

Blog post (how-to or experience report), document, or slides. For a series, the part number.

## The user's wording

Lines from the user's notes to keep as they are. Quote them.

## Real cases and numbers

Each story, number, and result the piece may use, with its source (notes, a link, a file). Anything not listed here is not used.

## Confidential items

What can't be named, and how to disguise it ("one client", "a retail project").

## Uncertain claims

Claims the user has flagged as uncertain. Only these get a caveat.

## Sibling pieces

Other posts in the series or related docs: their titles, the terms they use, and the numbers they state, so this piece matches them.

## Lines already rejected

Lines the user has turned down for this piece, so a later draft doesn't bring them back in new words.

## Decisions

Dated choices made while writing (heading style, scope, what's left out, and why).
