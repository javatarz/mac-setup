The engineers I admire most treat AI output as a first draft, never a final
answer. They review every line, question every assumption, and hold the
same bar they always have.

This approach works because it separates speed from judgment. Teams that
adopt it move faster without sacrificing quality. Teams that skip it
eventually pay the cost in production incidents.

The principle is simple: trust the tool to generate, trust the engineer to
decide. Organizations that internalize this distinction will outperform
those that treat AI as a shortcut around thinking.
